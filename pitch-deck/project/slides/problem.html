The problem

Clinical teams spend as much energy on paperwork as on patients

Fragmented tools

Referrals, intake, scheduling, billing and documentation live in systems that don't talk to each other.

Manual handoffs

Staff re-key the same patient information across the EMR, billing, and referral portals by hand.

AI bolted on, not built in

Most platforms offer AI as an add-on feature, announced long before it's something a clinic can run on.
