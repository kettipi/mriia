The field

Six kinds of competitor, one Canadian gap

Developer infrastructure

Medplum · Canvas Medical

Powerful building blocks — you still build and operate the workflow.

Care orchestration

Awell · OceanMD

Coordinates referrals and workflows, but isn't a full clinic operating system.

Practice management & EHR

Healthie · Jane · Elation · Accuro/QHR

Ready operational platforms — not agentic, and not built around AI.

AI point agents

Phelix AI · Cortico · Notable · Commure

Automate one task well — not the whole back office.

Canadian EMR incumbents

TELUS Health · OSCAR Pro · MEDFAR/MYLE

Own the install base — and are the slowest to rebuild around agentic AI.

Global enterprise & adjacent

Epic · athenahealth · Tebra · Fabric · Innovaccer

Global scale — Canadian workflows and provincial billing aren't their focus.

None of the six groups combines Canadian-ready deployment with agentic AI.
